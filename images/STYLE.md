# Book illustration style (Phase 0 — draft)

Flat editorial illustration for an open business/IT textbook. Not photoreal, not 3D, not cartoony.

**Palette:** warm coral and cool teal accents on a light cream background. Limit to 2–3 colors plus neutrals. Must read clearly in greyscale.

**Composition:** clean geometric shapes, soft edges, subtle gradients, generous negative space. No UI chrome with readable labels.

**Subject treatment:** simplified human figures without identifiable faces; screens show abstract shapes only (blobs, bars, bubbles)—never legible text or numbers.
